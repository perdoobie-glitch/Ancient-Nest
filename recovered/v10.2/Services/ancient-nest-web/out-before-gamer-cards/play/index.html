Combat styles / Game-type traits

Choose how the fight feels.

Game-type traits

Shield-based firefights

Longer science-fiction engagements reward sustained accuracy, coordinated pressure, and timely retreats. Rechargeable-shield traits create opportunities to recover, reposition, and challenge an opponent again.

Game-type traits

Armor & health combat

A more grounded tactical pace puts greater weight on exposure, positioning, and decisive shooting. Conventional weapons, armor, and health define Annu’s default combat identity.

Combat styles are controlled by game-type traits. By default, Annu uses conventional weapons, armor, and health; Naki uses advanced energy technology and rechargeable shields. Annu receives advanced capabilities only when the selected game type explicitly grants them.
