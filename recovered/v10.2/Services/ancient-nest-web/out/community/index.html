Community development / Your input matters

Be part of the change.

Specific feedback helps us make better decisions.

Tell us when a weapon feels satisfying, a route creates a memorable fight, or an encounter feels unfair. Reports about balance, performance, maps, and usability help guide each revision.

For bugs, include what happened, what you expected, and steps to reproduce it. For performance issues, include your hardware and settings. Explain what you enjoy as well as what needs work.

Playtesting, custom games, and Nest Forge give players room to experiment. Perdoobie & Mishwad Studio remains responsible for the game’s direction, with player experience helping shape its development.

Public playtest dates and official feedback channels will be announced when ready. No feedback form or public playtest registration is connected here yet.
