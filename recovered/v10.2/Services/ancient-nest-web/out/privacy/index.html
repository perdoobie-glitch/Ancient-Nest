Site information / Privacy

Privacy information.

Draft for review before public launch. This is not a completed legal policy.

What this service stores

When the account service is enabled, Steam supplies a verified Steam ID. This service stores that ID, an Ancient Nest account ID, username, session records and linked game public keys. Steam passwords are never requested by this website. Session cookies are Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax; game refresh credentials are stored hashed by the backend. Eligible matchmaking records include kills, deaths, assists, outcomes, rank points, performance and match history. They are supplied through the private matchmaking coordinator. The separate demo profile remains illustrative. Account deletion removes account-linked sessions, devices and stats; non-account match metadata may remain.

Hosting and external links

Your chosen hosting provider may process network requests and maintain access logs under its own policies. A configured Steam store link opens an external service with separate privacy practices.

Before public launch

Perdoobie & Mishwad Studio must review and publish an accurate privacy policy and contact details before launching account collection publicly or adding analytics. Review hosting logs, retention, processors, user rights and applicable jurisdictions with qualified advice.
