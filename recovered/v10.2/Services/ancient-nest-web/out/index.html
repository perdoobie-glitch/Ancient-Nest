01 / About the game

The past is sacred. The fight is yours.

An independent multiplayer FPS from Perdoobie & Mishwad Studio.

Ancient-world environments meet futuristic warfare. Choose longer shield-based firefights or grounded armor-and-health combat, with precise gunplay and customizable rules at the center.

We’re building Ancient Nest with community feedback in mind. Playtesting, custom games, and Nest Forge give players ways to help shape what comes next.

Defenders of the Ancient Nest. Annu possesses its gold but has not learned to harness it for advanced energy technology.
