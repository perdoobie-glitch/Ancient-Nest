Ancient Nest v0.6 audio credits

The original v0.5 oscillator/noise effects and Flite narration have been replaced.
No Halo game recordings or announcer impersonation are included.

Gun reports: The Free Firearm Sound Library by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson,
Kevin Heras and Matthew Nanney / Still North Media. CC0 1.0.
https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
Retrieved via https://github.com/petroulacl/fps-asset-kit .
Individual close reports were isolated from the original multiple-shot recordings,
trimmed into crack/tail layers, equalized and compressed.

Foley, footsteps, metal, shield/energy, explosions and UI sources:
Kenney / www.kenney.nl. Impact Sounds, RPG Audio, Sci-fi Sounds, Digital Audio
and Interface Sounds. CC0 1.0. Copied original license notices in audio/licenses.
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds
Retrieved via https://github.com/GeorgeQLe/assets-sfx-combat
and https://github.com/Mcamento8/open-game-sfx-index .

Short breath/pain/death texture: Zombies Sound Pack by artisticdude, CC0 1.0.
https://opengameart.org/content/zombies-sound-pack
Melee/throw air movement: Battle Sound Effects by Ogrebane, CC0 1.0.
https://opengameart.org/content/battle-sound-effects
Clips shortened, pitched and mixed; these are stylized texture cues.

Announcer: original Ancient Nest lines, newly synthesized offline with the generic
Piper LibriTTS-R medium voice (speaker 3922), with light compression.
Piper voice model / rhasspy: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices
Model card: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_US/libritts_r/medium/MODEL_CARD
LibriTTS-R dataset: https://www.openslr.org/141/ ; CC BY 4.0.
Attribution to the LibriTTS-R authors and contributors; license text is included.
Model conversion distributed by k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx:
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/tag/tts-models
The model and synthesis engines are authoring tools and are not shipped.
Original line text and generated game cues are included as editable audio assets.

Exact curated source paths, source identities, licenses and SHA-256 checksums are
recorded in audio/source/PROVENANCE.json. Source WAV clips and all mix recipes are
included, so Ogg files can be rebuilt with authoring/create_soundscape.py.

Menu music: Sanctuary at Dawn, original Ancient Nest composition.
Synthesized original instruments and melody, no borrowed recordings or game music.
Score: data/menu_music_score.json. Rebuild: authoring/create_menu_music.py.

Ancient Nest v14.4 weapon composites:
New files in audio/sfx/weapons/<weapon>/ mix the already-licensed close crack,
mechanical and tail variations listed above. No new external recordings or Halo
assets were added. Distant, first reload-stage and dry-fire clips are derived
from the corresponding existing weapon recordings. Rebuild with
python authoring/mix_weapon_reports.py (NumPy and ffmpeg required).


2026-10-15 user-supplied update: audio/announcer/killing_spree.ogg, killing_machine.ogg, in_the_building.ogg were provided in AncientNest_Codex_Update.zip. The package does not specify their producer or license. They are integrated as supplied, not attributed to the original generated audio recipes.

V15 menu track: Siege of the Nest, original synthesized composition at 128 BPM in D minor. No borrowed recordings or melodies. Rebuild with authoring/create_intense_menu.py. Old menu_music.ogg retained.

V16 active menu theme: The Siege Awakens, original 87.27-second composition at 132 BPM.
Sampled orchestral instruments rendered with FluidSynth and TimGM6mb by Tim Brechbill
and David Bolton. Synthetic battle cries and war drums were authored for this track.
This track and its score/source are GPL-2.0-only; license and SoundFont notices are
in audio/licenses/MenuTheme-GPL-2.txt and TimGM6mb-copyright.txt. Complete score/render
source and SoundFont are included in authoring/create_war_menu.py and authoring/soundfonts/.
No existing song or commercial game music was sampled. The v15 score is historical;
audio/music/menu_theme.ogg now contains the v16 composition.

V19 plasma firing/reload/dry-fire effects: original procedural audio generated for Ancient Nest. Source recipe: authoring/make_v19_plasma_audio.py. No external recordings used for these new effects.

V19.1 waterfall ambience: original procedurally synthesized periodic water-rush noise by OpenAI for Ancient Nest; no sampled third-party recordings.

V20 update objective arming/defuse/tick tones: original procedural synthesis for Ancient Nest, no third-party samples.

Naki shields-off beep: original procedural sine/harmonic pulse generated for Ancient Nest, 2026-10-17. No external samples.
